1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you enter

To create charts and readings, you enter birth details: name or label for the chart, date of birth, time of birth and place of birth, and any other details you choose to add (for example gender or notes). You may enter birth details of other people (such as family members); please do so only if you have their permission.

Notes, research work and exports you create in the App are stored on your device unless you choose to sync or share them.

1.2 Account information (optional)

If you sign in, we use Google Firebase Authentication. Depending on the method you choose, we receive:

  • Google sign-in: your name, email address, profile photo (if any) and a Google account identifier.
  • Email sign-in: your email address. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it in readable form.
  • A unique user ID created by Firebase for your account.

1.3 Palm images (Palmistry feature)

If you use the Palmistry feature, the App uses your device camera to capture an image of your palm. Palm images are used only to generate your palm reading. We do not use them to identify you, we do not use them for advertising, and we do not sell or share them with anyone for their own purposes. We do not keep palm images on our servers after your reading is produced. A palm image is saved on your device only if you choose to save it.

1.4 Purchase and subscription information

Subscriptions are purchased through Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment; we never receive your card, UPI or bank details. To manage your subscription we receive and store purchase information such as the product purchased, purchase date, subscription status, renewal/expiry date, country/currency of purchase, and a purchase token. We use RevenueCat to verify purchases and manage your subscription access; RevenueCat receives this purchase information together with your App user ID.

1.5 Usage and diagnostic information

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the App is used and to fix problems. These services automatically collect information such as:

  • App interactions (for example screens viewed and features used), first-open and session information;
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, app version), approximate country/region derived from IP address;
  • App instance identifiers and, where available on your device, the Android Advertising ID;
  • Crash logs and diagnostic data (the state of the App when a crash or error occurred).

We use this information only for analytics, performance and stability. We do not use it for advertising and we do not show ads in the App. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your Android settings.

1.6 Location

Place of birth is used to calculate the coordinates and time zone needed for astrological calculations. If you search for a place, the text you type may be sent to a location lookup service to find the matching place. If the App ever asks for your device's location (for example, to show calculations for where you are now), it will do so only with your permission, and you can refuse or turn it off at any time in your Android settings. The App works without device location.

1.7 Information we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, SMS messages, call logs, or precise background location, and we do not access your photos or files except those you choose to pick, save or export.

2. How we use information

  • To calculate charts, dashas, divisional charts and other astrological results, and to generate readings (including palm readings).
  • To create your account, sign you in, and sync your saved birth details across your devices if you choose.
  • To process and verify subscriptions and unlock the features you have paid for.
  • To create shareable cards and exports when you ask for them.
  • To keep the App working, fix crashes and improve features and performance.
  • To respond to your support requests and send important service messages (for example about your account or subscription).
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, and to comply with legal obligations.

Astrological readings are generated by calculation from the details you provide. We do not use your data to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

3. Where your data is stored

  • Without an account: your birth details, charts, notes and settings are stored only on your device.
  • With an account and sync turned on: your saved birth details (and related saved chart data) are also stored in Google Firebase cloud services so you can access them on other devices.
  • Subscription data is stored by Google Play, RevenueCat and, where needed, in our Firebase project.

6. How long we keep data

  • Data on your device: kept until you delete it in the App, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.
  • Account and synced data: kept while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your account and synced data within 30 days.
  • Palm images: not kept on our servers after your reading is produced.
  • Purchase records: kept as long as needed to provide your subscription and as required by tax, accounting and other laws. Google Play keeps its own records of your purchases.
  • Analytics and crash data: kept for the limited periods set in Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (crash data is normally kept for about 90 days), then deleted automatically.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Use without an account: sign-in is optional; only subscriptions require it.
  • Edit or delete charts: you can edit or delete saved birth details in the App at any time.
  • Delete your account: in the App (Settings → Account → Delete account) or through our account deletion page.
  • Cancel a subscription: in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting your account or uninstalling the App does not cancel a Google Play subscription by itself.
  • Permissions: you can turn off camera, notification or location access in Android settings.

This includes rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and US state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect processing done before you withdrew it. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority (in India, the Data Protection Board of India).

8. Security

Data sent between the App and our service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Cloud data is stored with Google Firebase, which encrypts data at rest, and access to it is restricted by security rules so that only you can read your own synced data. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information. If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children. It is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country). Users under 18 may use the App only with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Our service providers (such as Google and RevenueCat) may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the providers' standard contractual clauses.
