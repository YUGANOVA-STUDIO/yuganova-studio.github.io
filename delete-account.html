Delete your account

Yatmok Astrology (Android app) · by Yuga Nova

This page explains how to delete your Yatmok Astrology account and the data linked to it. The App is developed by Yuga Nova.

Option 1: Delete in the App

  1. Open Yatmok Astrology and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and synced data are deleted, and you are signed out.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the App installed, send us a request from the same email address you used to sign in (for Google sign-in, your Google account email):

To: yatmokastrology@gmail.com
Subject: Delete my Yatmok account
Message: Please delete my Yatmok Astrology account and data. My sign-in email is: your email

Email deletion request

We may reply to confirm the request comes from the account owner. We will confirm by email once deletion is complete, within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, email address, profile photo, user ID).
  • Birth details and chart data synced to the cloud.
  • Subscription link between your account and our systems (including your RevenueCat customer record).

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Purchase and transaction records needed for tax, accounting or legal reasons are kept only as long as the law requires. Google Play also keeps its own record of your purchases.
  • Analytics and crash data that is not linked to your name or email is deleted automatically under Firebase's retention periods (crash data normally within about 90 days).

Before you delete

  • Cancel your subscription first. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Data on your phone: charts saved only on your device are not stored by us. To remove them, delete them in the App, or uninstall the App / clear its storage.
  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Want to delete only some data and keep your account? You can delete individual saved charts in the App, or email us to ask for specific data to be deleted.
